What I Build

Automation & data

Tools that take repetitive data work off someone's plate: syncing repo metadata, auditing Apps Script projects, and turning GSA vendor price lists and Excel workbooks into clean, checked, ready-to-use records.

Desktop tools

Tools that run on your own machine, not in the cloud: a photo-scan stitching wizard, a message-archive cleaner and viewer, a video clip joiner — plus Windows utilities for sandboxes, compliance checks, and game-save backups.

Web

Web apps that mostly run in the browser: a customizable intranet dashboard, a landscaping business manager, a client-side billing analyzer, and JSON and candidate-evaluation tools — plus this site and a voxel world you walk through. Several work offline, with your data staying on your machine.

Data pipelines

Pipelines that capture data as it arrives or on a schedule: prediction-market order books and crypto feeds recorded to Parquet, and public feeds — procurement listings, store deals — scraped into deduped, queryable datasets.
