*Dead as Disco* is the rhythm game I keep coming back to in clips: a neon martial-arts brawler from Brain Jar Games where every punch, kick, and dodge lands on the beat, and you can import your own songs and fight to those too. It entered Early Access on Steam and the Epic Games Store on May 5, 2026, at $24.99, and it is holding around 97% positive from roughly 15,000 Steam reviews. The custom-music feature (called My Music) was live at launch, not a roadmap promise, and the studio is aiming for a 1.0 release in 2027. Consoles are planned, but there is no date — so for now, it is PC-only.

That leaves the obvious question: what do you play on Xbox or Android while you wait? This is the list I put together, and I checked every entry rather than trusting the first summary I was handed. Prices are as of November 2026; they change, and Game Pass membership changes too.

## TL;DR

- **On Xbox, start with Hi-Fi RUSH.** It is the closest thing to *Dead as Disco* in spirit, and it is on Game Pass.
- **On Android, start with Muse Dash** for the brawler feel, or **PULSEBOUND** if you want something free and current.
- **For your own music, Android is the answer.** Xbox has almost nothing here — the one real route is a *Rock Band 4* disc.
- **Two names from other lists did not survive checking:** "Pulse Riot" does not exist, and *Beat Hazard 2* never came to Xbox.

## Bring your own music

This is the feature that makes *Dead as Disco* unusual, and it is where the two platforms split hard. Consoles lock down the local file system, so real bring-your-own-music rhythm games barely exist on Xbox. Android's open file system means several games just read the files on your phone.

### On Xbox: the honest answer is almost nothing

- **Rock Band 4** is the one real route, and it takes some doing. Harmonix delisted it from the Xbox and PlayStation digital stores on October 5, 2025, when the ten-year music licenses expired. A physical disc still works — 65 songs on the disc — and Xbox's disc-to-digital feature, now rolling out to all players, converts a *Rock Band 4* disc into a digital license, so the disc does not have to stay in the drive. It is a band game, not a brawler, and it shines with instruments, but it is the only way to play your own library's worth of licensed tracks on the console.
- **Beat Hazard 2 is not on Xbox.** Its Open Mic mode — which listens to any audio source, including Spotify running in the background — is real, but it is a PC feature. The game never released on Xbox.
- **Crypt of the NecroDancer on Xbox** gives you the built-in soundtracks, including the remixed ones. Dropping in your own MP3s is the PC version's feature.

### On Android: this is where it actually works

| Game | How it uses your music | Get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Beat Hazard Ultra** | Point it at the music on your device and it builds a neon twin-stick shooter from it — lasers and explosions scale with the song. Old (last updated 2014–2015) and un-updated since, but still listed and still fun if you have local files. | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.coldbeamgames.beathazardultragooglefull) |
| **BEAT MP3 2.0** | Scans your phone's music library and auto-generates tap-and-hold note charts from any MP3 or FLAC, with multiple difficulties. | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.studio7775.BeatMP3v2) (Free) |
| **Cytoid** | Free, open-source community rhythm engine. Download thousands of player-made charts, or map your own songs in the built-in editor. | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=me.tigerhix.cytoid&hl=en_NZ) (Free) |

Two more worth knowing about: **Music Racer** (free, 5M+ installs) generates a race track from any local song you pick — it has not been updated on Google Play since 2020, but it is still listed. **Rhythmical** builds four-lane note highways from your own audio or video files, and it is actively maintained — the latest update landed November 13, 2026. One name to skip: **Malody**, the other community chart engine, was removed from Google Play in October 2024. Cytoid is the maintained alternative.

## What did not make the list

I cut three things that show up in other versions of this list:

- **"Pulse Riot: Rhythm Brawler" does not exist.** There is no such game on Google Play. The name turns up in a novel, a Roblox song ID, and a UI template — not a store listing. PULSEBOUND, above, is the real game in that slot.
- **Beatstar is gone.** The licensed-pop tapper went offline on October 31, 2025, and its Google Play listing returns not found (rechecked November 10, 2026). Do not hunt unofficial APKs for it.
- **Beat Hazard 2 on Xbox** never happened, as above — it is PC and iOS only.
